Research draftKobayashi geometry

Exact and approximate Kobayashi chains on strongly pseudoconvex domains

This project separates exact realization of the Kobayashi distance from approximation by holomorphic discs. It constructs a strongly pseudoconvex domain with a pair admitting no finite realizing chain. On each bounded C2 strongly pseudoconvex domain Ω, it shows that OΩ(ε−1) discs suffice for additive error ε, uniformly over all endpoint pairs. For its specific obstruction family, the number of discs can be reduced to O(log log(1/ε)).

The draft leaves the optimal approximation rate for the obstruction family open.

Research draftWith Bo Xiao

Continuous and Smooth Plurisubharmonic Exhaustions: Threefold Counterexamples and Surface Regularization

Starting from Koike’s modified Grauert example, this paper constructs Kähler and non-Kähler threefolds with locally Lipschitz plurisubharmonic exhaustions but no C2 plurisubharmonic exhaustion. It proves continuous-to-smooth regularization for divisor complements in compact Kähler surfaces, including smooth quasi-projective surfaces, and holomorphic convexity for continuously exhausted reduced pure n-dimensional spaces with a holomorphic map to ℂn−1 whose fibers all have dimension at most one.

The general regularization question in complex dimension two is not resolved by this work.

Research draftWith Zhuo Liu and Chenghao Qing

Optimal estimates for direct images: product descent and top-degree rigidity

The manuscript proves product descent of optimal L2 estimates for prescribed direct-image metrics in two extremal bidegree ranges, including singular semipositive product metrics with multiplier ideals and minimum weighted fiberwise norms. For arbitrary singular semipositive line metrics, a rigidity theorem gives non-product adjoint descent in top fiber degree q = n. It also identifies the smooth adjoint metric with the Mourougane–Takayama metric.

Counterexamples exclude unrestricted degreewise descent. The singular non-product result is specific to top adjoint degree; the stated geometric and metric hypotheses remain part of each theorem.

Research draftWith Zhuo Liu and Chenghao Qing

Le Potier isomorphisms for coherent sheaves on complex spaces

This manuscript develops a weighted canonical Le Potier isomorphism for coherent sheaves on reduced irreducible complex spaces, under semipositivity assumptions on a locally free modification. It also establishes the classical unweighted differential-form isomorphism for locally free sheaves over arbitrary complex spaces, including nonreduced bases.

Examples identify obstructions for general coherent sheaves and show why weighted differential-form formulas need additional hypotheses.

Research noteZhijian Chen

Nadel Vanishing on Singular Complex Spaces via the Grauert–Riemenschneider Canonical Sheaf

This note formulates Nadel vanishing on reduced, pure-dimensional, weakly pseudoconvex Kähler complex spaces using the weighted Grauert–Riemenschneider canonical sheaf. It describes the sheaf on a resolution and derives vanishing from stated weighted L2-Dolbeault and global ∂̄ estimates.

The proof retains positivity requirements near the singular locus. A cusp example explains the failure of a naive tensor-product formulation.

Survey / Problem collectionZhijian Chen

Some problems in complex variables

A 39-page survey collecting 139 numbered problems in several complex variables and complex geometry across seven subject areas. It brings together classical and contemporary questions, proof sketches for established results, partial results, and attributed remarks from the literature. The October 2026 update covers Oka manifolds, Kähler families, and analytic rational curves on Fano manifolds, with explicit AI credit and the hypotheses of recent preprints. It also restores selected source questions and broader formulations, with precise citations and 161 references.

The manuscript classifies 36 problems as closed, 52 as partially resolved, and 51 as wide-open. These categories refer to the precise formulations stated in the survey.
